# Levtek meeting notes   
### History   
- Jake and Hannes got to know each other 10y+ ago   
- Quit their jobs and started doing market reseach + product iterations + user testing on various types or rideable robots   
- Got some public funding to cover the first year and couple of prototypes   
- Fine-tuned some autonomy features, but realized that's putting the cart before the horse   
    - Doesn't make sense to build fancy demo's too early   
    - Rather build useful product form with autonomy first strategy   
   
   
### User-led automation   
- Common trap where robotic companies get stuck is trying to do full, fancy autonomy from day one. Real world hits and they get stuck in pilot+manual fixing hell   
- Instead, enable very robust (non fragile) robots that are useful even if autonomy doesn't work   
- Starting selling the cart with useful modes of operation from day 1:   
    - Transport stuff   
    - Ride it   
    - Have it follow you   
- Gather data and learn from users how to best implement/prioritize automation features. prototypes include:   
    - mapping of indoor space   
    - gesture recognition and following    
    - visual reasoning    
- Upsell customer subscriptions on autonomy features   
   
   
### Design and Manufacturing   
- Master the supply chain and have built relations with suppliers that can scale from demo's to small scale production to massive scale production (Jake speciality)   
- Currently assembly inhouse until ~100 units   
- Batteries, motors from China, the rest from EU/US   
   
   
### Moat   
- Hardware isn't the most important part, software/HCI will define success and drive value   
- IP protection against big corporates, building IP portfolio   
- Know how in supply chain and team   
- Distribution, creating data fly wheel   
   
   
### Jake   
- INdustrial design from NZ   
- Internship in NYC   
- Mech construction, prod engineer, for over 30 products, everything from consumer, to very deep tech products, in startups, and corporates   
- Built motorcycle company, for tuning and styling components   
- Take products from sketch to mass production   
   
   
### Hannes   
- Engineering physics, ML and robotics research at Sony   
- always been tinkering and building stuff in his garage with his two brothers (also engineering physicists)   
- Philosophy to research things but then immediately apply it   
    - Study control engineering → put together a drone controller   
    - Learn CAD → prototype something   
- Very enthusiastic and maybe slightly naive when it comes to sales and fund raising, very unstructured and non strategic   
- Hyperfocused on building a useful tool for customers while building a larger vision   
   
   
### Scaling   
- BOM Breakdown planned for when to scale assembly lines   
- Design for assembly vs. design for manufaturing process learnt at axis   
- Couple mass deployment of robots coupled with research collaborations with universities and    
   
   
### Vision   
- New wave of robotics company, enabling robotic working companion for people doing exhausting jobs   
- LevKart has massive potential, no issue selling 10s of thousands of them, but not necessarily the only formfactor that makes sense   
- Data flywheel will be built up for service technicians, manufacturing and logistics, and how they interact with robots. Could enable other autonomy add-ons to external platforms down the line.   
- Might build an own bigger, robotic lastenrad, but way too early to figure out now    
   
   
### Thesis   
- All robots will be tools, and required intelligence level depends on   how versatile the use cases are   
- There is a spectrum of robots between 100% general purpose physical AI  (e.g., humanoid) and ultra specific purpose (e.g., delivery robot)
