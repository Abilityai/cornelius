#    
From Alex Schubert (scifounders)   
One of the most interesting insights from JPM convos for me is that clinical trial analysis work is even more archaic than I thought.   
For every trial, 100s of bioinformaticians have to convert (physical) provider notes into SAS, a legacy and sometimes buggy statistics software, before they can even analyze the results.   
Most big biopharma run the same process and statistical teams are dying for better workflows.   
I think anyone who builds a modern solution to automate will do very well and help make clinical trials more efficient.
