# Air defense on the frontline in Ukraine   
Responsibility of 3rd Army Corps is 180km of frontline   
Corps are the linear ground formations on the line of combat contact → First to encounter air threats   
- Russia uses combinations of UAVs to clear paths for missiles, do ISR,    
- SAMs are too expensive, and we only have enough production for one night of defending against UAVs   
   
   
First Echelon is being breached (5km from line of combat contact)   
- Defense is UAS and mobile 3D radars   
    - Deploy UAS integrated with mobile 3D radar stations for frontline interception   
    - Optimized for Efficiency, mobility, response speed   
    - Prioritized SAMs for missiles   
- Using Robin radar (~20k USD for smallest) and UAV quad interceptors + remote detonation   
- Drone costs ~900USD, worth it to destroy Mavics (1.5kUSD)   
- Already put shotguns on their drones, but it's very hard to aim and time the shot, tried it using LiDAR, but it's very difficult to process in real time. Russians learnt to avoid already   
- Interceptor needs to be in the air min 15min in order to chase shaheds   
   
   
   
Third Echelon   
- 5-15 km   
- Using israeli and Ukrainian radars   
- If you don't destroy the ISR drones, then Shaheds will get you   
- Using fixed wing drones   
   
   
Operators are still involved and tracking radar signals, then handing over to optical tracking, then to drone operators   
Two possible solutions:   
1. Acoustic detection in the air   
2. Small radars (500m range)   
   
   
First and 2nd echelon: radar and drone operators are the same team, sitting next to each other   
3rd echelon: skymapa creates common operational picture and teams pick up and confirm kills   
   
Speed matters:   
- get ahead instead of catch up
