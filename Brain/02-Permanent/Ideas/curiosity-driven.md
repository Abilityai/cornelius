# Curiosity-driven   
Searching and exploring without a set goal in mind, driven by what you're curious about.    
This seems to be the base of many **great **discoveries of the scientific and creative world. Great ideas and innovation is born out of being curiosity-driven.    
   
It's not:
- Following your passion
- Being perpetually "interested" in many things but not going deep
