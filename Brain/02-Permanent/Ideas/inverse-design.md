# Inverse design   
Specify the properties desired, and then determine the design to achieve the properties or goals   
   
The process of inverse design typically involves three steps[5](https://www.centerforinversedesign.org/approach):   
1. Articulate target properties for a given desired functionality and develop a set of overarching "design principles".   
2. Based on the design principles, choose initial "classes of materials" and use theoretical methods, in parallel with experimental methods, to screen these classes for promising candidates.   
3. Use detailed theoretical and experimental investigations to scrutinize individual candidates in greater detail, and feed back the results of these investigations into a second optimization loop, if needed.
