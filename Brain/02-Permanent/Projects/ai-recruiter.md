# AI recruiter   
## Description   
*[Written by Claude]*   
An AI-powered recruitment automation system that handles the full recruitment pipeline - from sourcing candidates to scheduling interviews.   
## What is it for   
Automating repetitive recruitment tasks to allow recruiters to focus on high-value activities like candidate relationships and closing.   
## Goals   
- **Sourcing**: Automated candidate discovery from multiple channels   
- **Screening**: AI-powered resume parsing and qualification scoring   
- **Outreach**: Personalized candidate communication at scale   
- **Scheduling**: Automated interview coordination across calendars   
- **Pipeline management**: Status tracking and follow-up automation   
   
## Measure of success   
- Time-to-hire reduction   
- Recruiter productivity (candidates processed per recruiter)   
- Candidate experience scores   
- Quality of hire metrics   
   
## Potential blockers   
- Integration with existing ATS/HR systems   
- Compliance with hiring regulations and bias concerns   
- Candidate trust in AI-driven processes   
- Email deliverability for outreach   
   
## MVP Scope   
1. Resume parsing and skill extraction   
2. Candidate scoring against job requirements   
3. Automated outreach sequences   
4. Calendar integration for scheduling   
   
## Technical approach   
- LLM for resume understanding and communication   
- Integration with LinkedIn, job boards, ATS   
- Calendar API integration (Google, Outlook)   
- Workflow engine for multi-step sequences
