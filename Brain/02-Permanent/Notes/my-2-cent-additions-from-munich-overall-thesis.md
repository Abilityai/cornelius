# My 2-cent additions from Munich:   
   
**Overall thesis**   
- CONFIRMED! There are brilliant people, and with the right mindset change/reinforcement they can lead Europe's tech sector in a few years   
   
   
**Topics/people at dinners:**   
- mostly really good, though quite some less contrarian "vertical AI" takes which won't lead to much groundbreaking stuff imo   
- Double-down on the industries and knowledge we have in Europe (manufacturing, biotech, defense, photonics, etc.) and that are truly important   
- Standing/mingling dinner is much better   
- Should we set up a signal gc for everyone who attended to remain in contact?   
   
   
**Culture**   
- had a weird vibe of competitiveness/exclusivity from some of the US VC guys, as in not seeming curious, open-minded, Europe-bullish. Doesn't add to the right type of ambiance and culture we should encourage. Might be a personality thing, or EUvsUS, or misconception from my end   
- 100% long-time, positive-sum, infinite game   
- There should be a strict no assholes policy, it only takes one to sour the whole batch and turn people off (n.b. special personalities or disagreeableness =/= assholes)   
   
   
**Gaining more applications (from the right people)**   
- I don't think general press is helpful right now, earliest once the first cohort and sponsors are selected to have something to show   
- Organizing or infiltrating hackathons and topical meetups that attract curious/strange/smart people is a great marketing stategy   
- I think we can better reach talent pools outside of our current networks by going through existing clubs and hacker houses (fr8, robotics club, certain labs at uni's, etc.) and select according to diversity of thought and experience (japanese 18y.o. moving to paris to work in a kitchen and then building prosthetics company)   
- Issue nerd-sniping challenges/publish about some of the topics people could be curious about   
   
   
**Terms/VCs**   
- It's a foregone conclusion that all fellows will need to or should raise VC money. Most will, but founders will need mentorship and community guidance to not get pulled into that game too early and see alternatives   
- uncapped SAFE notes only pre demo-day, to facilitate fundraising afterwards? Should not be a focus during the fellowship   
- Biggest round + highest valuation is not always the most founder friendly, long-term
