#    
World computer (aka smart contract chains/ethereum) means digitally reproducing, simulating every complexity of inventing a new computer. We’re not building s as new internet, we’re creating a new computer from scratch.   
I know the tech is important, but it occurred to me again how early and very very far from applications we are still when listening to   
[[Zero knowledge podcast - ep 311 launch of Celestia]]    
 where they’re explaining celestia’s place in the ecosystem of roll ups and settlement layers. Celestia is providing opportunities for making use of existing solutions, like settlement of assets, execution of logic, storage of data, and consensus finding. This reminded me of how traditional computers work at a hardware level and back to how they were invented.   
   
We started out with memory (tablets of knowledge), then millennia later, programmable logic (jacquard machines, punch cards), and work on how to adapt this into practical applications took hundreds of years. Within this new space, which I don’t want to call crypto/web3/ethereum or blockchains, as they’re all just pieces of the big picture, we’ve built rudimentary, tablet-level (see inscriptions) systems, that work, but need improving to be practical.
